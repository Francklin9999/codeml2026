## 1. Context and evidence

The rubric expects at least two contradictions explained by authority or date, including one in a plan or risk register. Known analysis notes Plan v3's 15 October date and risk register R-01 as stale against the later committee-approved conditional 22 October date. Strategy 3 defines a resolver; this idea evaluates whether the explanation itself is transparent and source-grounded.

## 2. Idea and novelty

For each chosen contradiction, publish a compact resolution card with both claims, source date versus information date, authority rationale, chosen current state, and residual uncertainty. Unlike strategy 3's general authority × time rules, this requires a human-readable explanation that could be challenged by the jury.

## 3. Rubric

Makes contradiction resolution inspectable rather than hiding the losing claim or treating recency as automatic truth.

## 4. Implementation

Create `work/strat28/cards.md`; include the go-live conflict and one risk-register conflict only after locator verification. Review each card against its underlying passage and distinguish superseded source from false source.

## 5. Experiment and decision

Baseline: identified contradictions in the draft answer set. Cold reviewer reads each card and identifies both claims, why one governs, and what remains unresolved. Adopt if 2/2 required contradictions are explained correctly by both reviewers and no current claim relies solely on a stale source.

## 6. Risks
