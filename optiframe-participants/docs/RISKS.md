# OptiFrame: risks

> **Purpose:** what can cost points, how we see it coming, and what we do instead. At most 15 rows.
> **Sources:** [`WINNING_PLAN.md`](WINNING_PLAN.md) §3 (where teams lose points) and §8 (kill criteria, quoted with their hour), the `TO MEASURE` items of the briefs in [`../agents/`](../agents/README.md) and of [`../agents/WAVE1_INTERFACES.md`](../agents/WAVE1_INTERFACES.md), plan B of [`../strat19.md`](../strat19.md) and [`../strat11.md`](../strat11.md).
> **Roles:** L = lead, V = vision, D = data and AI, F = frame. `H+n` = hours after the start. Thresholds are those of [`TEST_PLAN.md`](TEST_PLAN.md) §3; no measured value appears here.

| # | Risk | Early sign | Mitigation | Fallback | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The app does not open, or cannot use the camera, on the jury's phone (up to 65 points) | No public URL yet; gate G0 fails; the camera sheet does not open on the iPhone; cancelling the picker leaves the app waiting | Enable GitHub Pages now; test on a real iPhone and a real Android after every merge; "Importer une photo" as the second button | Demo-day order: mobile hotspot, then a team phone already open on the app, then the backup video. Never debug live | L |
| 2 | In-browser processing does not run on one of the two phones. **Kill criterion H+3:** marker detection does not run in the browser on both phones | `LOAD_FAILED` on a phone; OpenCV.js never reaches "ready"; memory crash on a large photo | Worker and warm-up from the home screen; photo downscaled before detection | Decide in 30 minutes: switch the marker library (`js-aruco2` is installed), or **plan B, strat19:** a Python server with the same steps, triggered only if in-browser processing fails gate G1 on one of the two phones; its URL must then stay up until the end of deliberations | L |
| 3 | Wrong scale: the printer scaled the reference sheet (10 to 30 points) | The 100 mm ruler does not measure 100 mm; a printed 50.0 mm shape is outside 50.0 ± 0.2 mm in the app | Print at 100 %, measure the ruler, `rig/set_print_scale.py <measured mm>`, rebuild and redeploy | Spare sheets already verified in the kit; reprint with other settings if `printScale` is outside 0.97 to 1.03 | V |
| 4 | The rig does not make the edge visible, or cannot be set up in 2 minutes. **Kill criterion H+4:** the backlit rig gives no better edge contrast than a plain background | Low `Mask.score`; moiré or a dim screen in the rectified image; a stranger needs help | Screen brightness at maximum, tracing paper against moiré; one-page instruction sheet; stranger test at G3 | Take the simpler rig: simplicity wins the two-minute setup | V |
| 5 | The transparent lens is not segmented (up to 30 points); the classic segmenter refuses faint rims | `NO_LENS` on clear or thin-edged lenses; failure rate above 5 % in the accuracy report | Backlight; model fallback when the classic mask is missing or weak (`LOW_MASK_SCORE`) | State it as a limit; the app asks for a new photo with a sentence; put the lens on a brighter backlight | V, D |
| 6 | A and B measured along another axis than the jury's calliper (5 to 15 points) | Error on B grows when the lens is turned; frequent `LENS_ROTATED` warnings; mentor question 3 unanswered | Guide line on the sheet; rotation warning above 5°; state the convention before the jury compares | Ask the jury to align the lens on the guide line and retake | V |
| 7 | Parallax and bias: the edge is a few mm above the sheet; without `focal35mm` in the EXIF no parallax correction is applied (3 to 8 points) | Constant positive bias in the Bland-Altman rows of the report; no `cameraDistMm` on a phone | Phone flat at 35 to 45 cm; bias fitted with leave-one-lens-out and written only when it helps | Keep the identity `bias.json`; never ship a correction that was not checked on unseen lenses | V |
| 8 | One bad photo ruins one of only two measurements; hand-held shots are not repeatable (5 to 15 points) | Spread of A or B above 0.6 mm; `INCONSISTENT_SHOTS` often; the same lens gives different numbers on two tries | Quality gates that refuse the photo; up to 3 shots fused, outliers dropped | **Plan B, strat11:** a cardboard phone stand that fixes height and parallelism, built only if hand-held shots fail the repeatability target | V |
| 9 | Accuracy gate missed. **Kill criteria H+8:** no end-to-end measurement on a real lens. **H+14:** MAE still above 1.5 mm | No filled row in TEST_PLAN §6.2; G1 or G2 not passed | Calliper readings and validation photos first (they need no code change); `eval.html` and `tools/accuracy_report.py` | H+8: D and F stop and help V. H+14: stop adding features to measurement, find the largest term of the error budget with printed test shapes, fix that one | V |
| 10 | Quality thresholds are provisional (set on synthetic scenes): the app refuses good photos or accepts bad ones; mounted glasses from the jury behave unpredictably | `BLURRY` on sharp photos; a wrong contour accepted; an unexpected message on the stand's mounted pairs | Shoot the wanted failures and the mounted pairs early; move a constant only with a real photo as evidence | Loosen the one threshold that blocks the demo pair and state the limit; mounted glasses must give a sentence, not a crash | V |
| 11 | "Données et IA" has no trained model and no metrics (up to 15 points). **Kill criterion H+18:** the model is not better than the non-AI path on hard photos, or is too slow on a phone | No dataset at H+8; `lens_seg.onnx` absent; condition names of the collection page (`lampL`, `lampT`, `lampR`, `colour`) not accepted by `autolabel.py` (`lamp1..3`, `color`); no merge step between the real and synthetic folders | Paired capture and synthetic generator; align the names before shooting; train on Colab; ablation table | The model is documented, not shipped: the data file shows the dataset, the method and the honest metrics, and the app keeps the non-AI path | D |
| 12 | The STL is invalid in a slicer, or the lens does not fit the printed rim (up to 10 points). **Kill criterion H+18:** no print started | `tools/validate_stl.py` fails; slicer warnings; the lens falls out or cannot be pushed in | Automatic mesh check in the tests; slicer check on a real pair; one printed rim to tune clearance and lips | H+18: drop the "real print" bonus; keep the slicer screenshot and the overlay with the gap in mm as evidence | F |
| 13 | The SVG is not at true scale once printed (up to 5 points) | Printed scale bar outside 50.0 ± 0.2 mm; the lens does not match its outline | Dimensions in mm in the SVG, printed scale bar, a real print test from Chrome and from Safari | Print instruction "100 %, actual size" said aloud; strat15's 1:1 PDF only if the SVG test fails on the jury's printer | F |
| 14 | The pair takes more than 30 s, or the first load is too slow on mobile data (13.3 MB of OpenCV.js) | "Pas à pas" timings near the budget; long "loading" state on 4G; stale version served by the service worker | Warm-up in the worker; cache-first vendor files; size budget on the initial JavaScript; bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` on each change | Open the app before the jury arrives; `?nosw=1` clears a stale cache; fewer shots per lens | L |
| 15 | The demo improvises, hides its limits, or shows unmerged work (up to 10 points). **Kill criterion H+20:** any feature not merged does not exist | No timed rehearsal; `À COMPLÉTER` left in the demo script; a deployment in the last 30 minutes | Feature freeze and last-known-good tag at G5; three timed rehearsals; limits slide filled from the validation study | Redeploy the last-known-good tag; backup video; skip the data slide, never the limits | L |
